TheZiess Method - TikTok FPS Bitrate Fallback V18

- Real MP4/TikTok FPS still has priority.
- Captions and hashtags are never used.
- When actual FPS is unavailable, the checker estimates from bitrate:
  * under 2 Mbps -> 30 FPS
  * 2 Mbps to under 16 Mbps -> 60 FPS
  * 16 Mbps and above -> 600 FPS
- Estimated values are visibly labelled "Estimated from bitrate".
- Bitrate-based FPS is a product heuristic, not measured frame-rate metadata.
