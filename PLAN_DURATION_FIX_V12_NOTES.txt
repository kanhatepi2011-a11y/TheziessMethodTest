TheZiess Method - Plan Duration Fix V12

Plan durations:
- PRO: 30 days
- PREMIUM: 180 days
- MAX: 1 year (365 days)

Security and behavior:
- PRO, PREMIUM and MAX remain admin-only assignments.
- Website users cannot claim paid plans for free.
- All paid plans now require a real expiration date.
- Existing unlimited MAX subscriptions are migrated to 365 days from starts_at.
- Existing PREMIUM subscriptions shorter than 180 days are extended to 180 days from starts_at.
- Frontend, API session checks, admin statistics and Telegram messages all use expiry dates.
